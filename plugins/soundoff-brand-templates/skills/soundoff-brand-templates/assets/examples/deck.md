# Results

## Where the leads came from

Three shows produced 80% of qualified leads.

| Show | Qualified leads | Cost per lead |

| Show A | 214 | $38 |
| Show B | 167 | $44 |
| Show C | 121 | $51 |

# 2027 Plan

## What changes

- Drop one regional show with the highest cost per lead
- Add product demos on the hour at the two largest shows
- Route qualified leads to Sales within 48 hours

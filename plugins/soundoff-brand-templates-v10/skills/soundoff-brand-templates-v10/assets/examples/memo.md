Open enrollment for 2027 benefits runs **November 18 through December 6**.
Elections made during this window take effect January 1.

## What to do

1. Review your current elections in the benefits portal.
2. Make any changes before December 6.
3. Print or save the confirmation page.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you make no changes, your current medical, dental, and vision elections
> roll over. Flexible spending elections do not roll over and must be made again.

Questions go to the HR office or your manager.
